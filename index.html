HOW TO PLAY

  • A word is hidden under the tiles. You have 60 seconds to crack it. Clock hits zero = run over.
  • Unlimited guesses. Type, then hit SPACE to submit.
  • Only whole words count: type every letter, then SPACE. Wrong words are free (unless HARDCORE).
  • Nail the whole word on your first guess for a FIRST TRY!
  • Type the whole word fast and watch the KPS meter catch fire.
  • The CLUE BOX starts with 1 clue. Another unlocks every 15s of clock used (penalties speed that up).
  • FLAWLESS counts words solved in a row without a wrong guess.
  • Every 5 words: harder tier + pick a perk. Hold 3. 123: press once to inspect, again to use.
  • BACKSPACE deletes · CTRL+BACKSPACE clears · ESC pause.
  • Every tier you clear adds flair to your skull. When you die it goes in the trophy case.
